QUESTIONS

Before you start the trial

Do I need to send my data anywhere?

No. fastQuote runs entirely on your machine. Your price book, vendor costs, and job files stay local. There is no fastQuote cloud account and no data collection of any kind.

Do I have to use AI?

No. The core pipeline — BOM parsing, cost roll-up, markup, quote sheet, validation — is fully deterministic and works without any API key. AI (drawing takeoff, document reading) is optional and, when used, runs on your provider account.

Will it work with my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. fastQuote reads and writes standard Excel and LibreOffice workbooks. Your BOM and price book stay as flat files you can open, edit, and back up like any other spreadsheet.

Can it read my drawings?

Yes — PDF BOM tables, DXF cut profiles, and STEP solid models. The 4-layer takeoff engine extracts, checks, and prices rows into your BOM, and every assembly drill-down asks before it goes deeper. Deterministic checks decide what is trusted — never a model's self-confidence.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

Your workbooks keep working. To keep generating new quotes, activate a license — the numbers are on the pricing page. No auto-billing traps, no upgrade fees.

Is this an ERP?

No. fastQuote is quoting-first: it owns the BOM-to-quote path and produces ERP-ready output you can import. Shops that want the full loop — purchase orders, receipts, invoices, labor, live job cost — get it in the Enterprise tier, built on the same local-first engine.

What if I don't like it?

Cancel within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. No forms, no questions.
