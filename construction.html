THE BID

The parts of a bid spreadsheets get wrong

Per discipline

Your costs, your markups

  • Material, labor, equipment, subcontract, permits and consumables as their own cost types
  • Overhead and profit set per cost type — the markup is calculated, not typed
  • Waste factor per cost type, applied to unit cost
  • CSI code on every row; units in LF, SF, SY, CY, SQ, LS and more
MATLLABREQUIPSUBCPERMCONS
On the summary

Project-level costs, done right

  • General conditions and other project costs as a lump sum, % of direct, % of sell, or hours × rate
  • Show each one as its own line, or spread it across the disciplines to the cent
  • Contingency on the whole job; bond as a share of the contract, grossed up
  • One combined quote PDF: a section per discipline and the total contract value
